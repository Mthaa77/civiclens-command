# CivicLens Premium Redesign Brief

## Mode
Redesign · Preserve. The navigation, civic reporting flow, private case storage, source/provenance model, and launch geography remain intact. The visual language is being upgraded rather than the product contract being rewritten.

## Preserve
- Home, Report, My Government, Community, My Cases, and Learn routes.
- Private-by-default case and evidence behavior.
- Official/community/CivicLens provenance distinctions.
- Gauteng and City of Tshwane launch context.
- Existing AsyncStorage key and case interaction model.

## Improve
- Editorial hierarchy, typography, and line-length control.
- Shared tokens for color, radii, spacing, elevation, and state feedback.
- Premium card composition and action emphasis.
- Copy clarity: shorter labels, stronger headings, more decisive next-step language.
- Native mobile readability and cross-screen consistency.

## Remove
- Flat template-like surfaces and low-contrast default scaffolding.
- Overly generic button/card language where a civic action can be more specific.
- Decorative elements that do not clarify trust, place, responsibility, or next action.

## Protected contracts
- Route structure and primary tab labels.
- Civic data model and local persistence behavior.
- Privacy, provenance, and source-freshness semantics.
- Report wizard progression and case save behavior.

## Design read
- Audience: residents first; community organisers and learners second.
- Visual language: editorial civic premium; calm, authoritative, warm, practical.
- Motion: restrained 80–220ms ease-out feedback only.
- Density: medium-low on entry screens; medium on data surfaces.
- Typeface pairing: Space Grotesk display + Inter body.
- Rhythm: 8pt spacing system.
